# Resigned Batch Update
- Added `Resigned` as a Batch Attendance status.
- The first day marked Resigned becomes the trainee's resignation day.
- Resigned trainees remain visible on the resignation day and all historical days, preserving attendance, quiz, typing, role play and other recorded history.
- They are hidden from later Attendance, Quiz and Typing Test days.
- Quiz/Typing saves and imports skip days after resignation.
- Batch summary includes a clickable Resigned card with resignation day/date and recorded-data counts.
- New hires/Agents summary count excludes resigned trainees, while the underlying historical trainee record is preserved.
